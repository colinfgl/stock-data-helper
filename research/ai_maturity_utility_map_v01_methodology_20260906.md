# AI Maturity & Utility Map v0.1

Date: 2026-09-06
Project: 存股作戰地圖
Status: Research / Shadow / 0 formal weight

## Purpose
Before mapping AI into investable industries, first test whether the AI capability is mature enough, reliable enough, useful enough in real-world conditions, economically justified, scalable, and already adopted by paying users. This prevents the chain: "AI demo -> industry boom -> stock upgrade" from skipping evidence gates.

## Six scoring dimensions (0-5)
1. Capability: Can the system complete the target task at the required technical level?
2. Reliability: Does it work repeatedly across normal and adverse conditions without frequent intervention?
3. Real-world utility: Does performance survive outside lab/demo environments and solve a real user/business pain point?
4. Economics: Is total cost of ownership competitive with labor, traditional automation, or incumbent workflows?
5. Scalability: Can deployment expand from tens to thousands of units/users without cost, support, infrastructure or safety breaking down?
6. Commercial adoption: Are there paying customers, repeat orders, renewals, or measurable production deployment?

Readiness score = simple average of the six dimensions in v0.1. Do not tune weights after seeing stock performance.

## Maturity stages
- M0 Concept: idea / research hypothesis only.
- M1 Research / Prototype: technical feasibility shown, but no durable field use.
- M2 Pilot / Limited Deployment: real users or facilities, but small scale and bounded setting.
- M3 Constrained Commercial: paying deployment exists, but use case / geography / workflow remains constrained.
- M4 Scaled Bounded Commercial: large-scale real-world deployment inside clearly defined operating conditions.
- M5 Production Platform: broad production infrastructure with repeatable economics and measurable revenue / EPS contribution.

## Safety Criticality (1-5)
1 = errors are low consequence.
5 = errors can cause injury, death, major financial or public-safety harm.

Higher safety criticality raises the required Reliability score and evidence standard. A 99% success rate may be excellent for recommendation software but unacceptable for guide robots, autonomous driving, or medical decision systems if the residual failures are hazardous.

## Investment translation gate
AI maturity does not automatically create stock alpha. Evidence must continue through:
Capability -> Field utility -> Unit economics -> Deployment -> Orders -> Production -> Revenue share -> Gross margin / EPS -> Valuation.

A stock cannot be upgraded only because its industry has a high AI maturity score. Company-specific revenue and margin conversion still must be verified.

## v0.1 portfolio/industry interpretation
- AI Server / AI Infrastructure: M5. Direct earnings and capacity evidence available.
- Generative AI: M4. High adoption and economic value; reliability still requires human review in many workflows.
- Edge / On-device AI: M4. Hardware/model capability strong; application monetization is still spreading.
- L4 autonomous driving in geofenced ODD: M4. Real commercial deployment, but safety and ODD constraints remain central.
- Industrial AGV/AMR: M4. Structured factory/logistics settings are mature.
- CPO / silicon-photonics switching: M3. First production exists, but broad deployment and Taiwan supplier revenue conversion remain to be proven.
- Quadruped / wheeled-leg robots: M3. Useful in inspection / special environments, but scene-constrained.
- Agentic AI: M3. Technical progress is fast, but enterprise deployment is still early and long-horizon reliability is a gate.
- Guide robot / robotic guide dog: M2. Real pilots exist; safety, liability, economics and large-scale support are unproven.
- Humanoid robots: M2. Factory pilots can perform repeatable work, but generality, economics, safety and maintenance remain early.
- Quantum + AI: M1. Research / workflow integration is advancing, but broad commercial advantage and fault-tolerant quantum remain early.

## Governance
- Formal model weight: 0.
- Do not change P4 or existing frozen probabilities.
- Scores should be changed only when new evidence changes one of the six dimensions.
- Record evidence date and source for every upgrade/downgrade.
- Never convert technology maturity directly into a buy signal.
